# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Users

Football fans in Ghana shopping across national team shirts, club team shirts, and retro shirts.

## Product Purpose

Jersey Haven helps fans discover, compare, and select football shirts, then begin checkout using Ghanaian payment methods.

## Positioning

The shop groups football shirts into three collections: National teams, Club teams, and Retro.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The requested interface is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Catalog filters should make the different kit types easy to browse. Checkout creates an order reference for manual Mobile Money payment and offers cash on delivery. The storefront and product catalog remain illustrative demo content; merchant MoMo details, inventory, shipping rules, and a production catalog must be configured by the shop owner. The shop confirms delivery coverage and manually verifies payments.

## Brand Commitments

The shop is named Jersey Haven.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing logo, product photography, live catalog, payment credentials, or business policies were supplied. Product names, prices, descriptions, and imagery used in the demo must not imply verified stock or commercial terms.

## Product Principles

- Organize shirts into National teams, Club teams, and Retro.
- Make product discovery fast through useful filters.
- Show product and price details before checkout.
- Keep Ghanaian payment choices visible and understandable.
- Clearly identify illustrative store data and demo checkout behavior.
